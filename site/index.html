How Billcast works

The odds come from a tested statistical model that runs in your browser. Muse Spark does the reading and writing around it, and never sets the number. Trained on .

  1. Read

    Muse Spark turns a digest into model inputs and quotes the phrase behind each fiscal flag.

    Muse Spark 1.3
  2. Score

    Five logistic regressions, one per step, trained on every bill since 2007 and checked against a session they never saw.

    Statistical model
  3. Explain

    Muse Spark reads the step odds and factor weights and says why, in plain English.

    Muse Spark 1.3
  4. Rewrite

    Muse Spark drafts amendments aimed at the weak steps. The model re-scores them, so you see the real change.

    Muse Spark + model
